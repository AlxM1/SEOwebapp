### Tax season shouldn't be a nightmare.

If your business runs on Stripe subscriptions, you already know the drill: end of quarter rolls around, your accountant asks for a payment export, you realize you've never actually synced your Stripe dashboard to QuickBooks, and the next three hours vanish into a spreadsheet you'll never want to look at again.

Missed transactions. Wrong categories. Duplicate entries from manual copy-paste. Revenue that doesn't reconcile with your bank. It's the kind of bookkeeping chaos that costs you money — in accountant hours, in tax preparation time, and occasionally in actual penalties.

**There's a better way.**

### What StripeSync for SEOh Does

StripeSync for SEOh is a direct bridge between your Stripe billing account and QuickBooks Online. The moment a customer pays, renews, or cancels, that transaction is reflected in your books — automatically, accurately, and without a single spreadsheet.

Every sale appears in QuickBooks within seconds, already categorized under your income account, already labeled with the customer email and subscription tier, already dated correctly. Your accountant opens QuickBooks and everything is there. You never have to think about it.

Refunds create Credit Memos automatically. Cancellations are logged. Nothing slips through.

---

### Key Features

**Real-time Sales Receipts** — Every successful Stripe payment generates a QuickBooks Sales Receipt immediately. Not at the end of the day. Not on a schedule. The moment the payment clears.

**Accurate Income Categorization** — Transactions map directly to your chosen QuickBooks income account. Configure it once, and every sale lands in the right bucket — ready for tax time, ready for investor reporting, ready for your P&L.

**Automatic Refund Handling** — Refunds and cancellations create Credit Memos in QuickBooks automatically, keeping your books balanced without manual intervention.

**Zero Duplicate Entries** — StripeSync tracks every transaction it has synced. If Stripe fires a webhook twice (it happens), QuickBooks only gets one entry. Your books stay clean.

**Full Subscription Lifecycle** — Payments, renewals, upgrades, cancellations — the entire billing lifecycle maps to the right QuickBooks entries, so your revenue story is always complete.

### How It Works

**Step 1 — Connect**
Log into your SEOh admin dashboard and navigate to the QuickBooks integration page. Click "Connect to QuickBooks."

**Step 2 — Authorize**
You'll be redirected to Intuit's secure authorization page. Log in with your QuickBooks account and approve access. No passwords are ever shared with StripeSync — the connection uses Intuit's official OAuth 2.0 protocol.

**Step 3 — Done**
That's it. From this point forward, every Stripe payment flows directly into QuickBooks. No cron jobs to configure, no exports to schedule, no manual work to do.

---

### Why Trust It?

**Security you can rely on.** StripeSync never stores your QuickBooks password. Authentication uses OAuth 2.0 — the same protocol used by every major integration in the Intuit ecosystem. Your credentials stay with Intuit.

**Built on webhooks, not polling.** Unlike integrations that check for new payments every few hours, StripeSync listens directly to Stripe's webhook events. When a payment happens, QuickBooks is updated within seconds — not hours.

**Accurate to your chart of accounts.** You choose the QuickBooks income account. Every transaction lands exactly where you want it, with the right description, the right date, and the right amount. No re-categorizing, no cleanup.

**Idempotent by design.** Webhooks occasionally fire twice. Banks retry transactions. StripeSync handles all of it — each Stripe event is tracked and never creates a duplicate QB entry.
